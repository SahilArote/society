Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to questions frequently asked by residents, security guards, and society committee members.

How does the guard notify me when a delivery person arrives?
When a delivery agent arrives at the security gate, the guard selects your flat number, inputs the delivery company (e.g., Zomato, Swiggy, Amazon, Blinkit), and captures a verification photo. Within less than a second, your smartphone receives a high-priority push notification with sound and vibration. You simply tap Allow Entry or Deny Entry directly from your screen.
Can multiple family members in the same flat approve visitors?
Yes! Through the Household Management section, the primary flat owner can add family members by mobile number. All registered household members receive incoming visitor alerts simultaneously, ensuring no visitor is left waiting if one person is in a meeting or away from their phone.
What is a Digital Pass and how does it work?
A Digital Pass is a pre-approved entry invite with an encrypted QR code. You can create one for guests or service technicians arriving on a specific day and share it directly via WhatsApp. When your guest arrives at the gate, the guard scans the QR code for instant entry clearance without calling you.
Who owns and operates NexGate & GreenGate?
NexGate & GreenGate are developed, maintained, and operated by NexBuild Technologies, based in Mumbai, Maharashtra, India. We partner with registered housing societies, residential welfare associations (RWAs), and property developers to provide state-of-the-art society access technology.
How do I delete my account or personal information?
You can request full account and data deletion at any time by emailing our dedicated privacy desk at helpdesk@nex-build.com with your registered mobile number and apartment unit details.
