1. Introduction

Welcome to the Privacy Policy for Nexbuil Technologies. We are committed to protecting personal privacy and ensuring transparency regarding how data is handled across our mobile applications.

Our software platform facilitates security communication, gatekeeper operations, visitor approval workflows, and household records between gated residential housing societies, flat residents, and on-duty security gate personnel.

This Privacy Policy governs your use of our two dedicated Android applications published on the Google Play Store:

  • NexGate Resident App — Designed for apartment owners and tenants residing within a gated society.
  • GreenGate Guard App — Designed for security guards operating on-duty gate checkpoints and kiosks.
Scope of This Policy: This document outlines our data practices strictly based on the technical implementation of our codebase. We collect and process only the minimal necessary data required to deliver gated security and society management features.

2. Applications Covered by This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies equally to the following two distinct Android applications developed and maintained by us. While both applications communicate with our central secure backend, their features, required permissions, and user categories differ as detailed below.

App 1 NexGate Resident
com.greengate.guard.greengate_guard_app

Primary Purpose: Serves as the personal residential portal for apartment residents. Enables residents to receive real-time gate visitor notifications (with security photos taken at the gate), approve or deny entry requests, manage household family members, register household vehicles, and view society notices.

Target Users: Apartment owners, family members, and verified tenants within registered residential housing societies.

App 2 NexGate Guard
com.nexbuild.nexguard

Primary Purpose: Serves as the security gate checkpoint terminal and mobile app. Enables on-duty security guards to register arriving visitors, capture a front-gate visitor security photograph, look up the recipient flat, transmit real-time arrival approval requests to residents, and record visitor check-ins and check-outs.

Target Users: Authorized security personnel, security agency guards, and estate gatekeepers deployed at residential society entrance gates.

4. Information Collected Automatically

When you interact with our applications, our servers automatically collect minimal technical metadata to maintain operational security, prevent unauthorized access, and ensure server uptime:

  • IP Address & Network Status: Recorded in backend security audit logs alongside administrative actions and visitor request transactions.
  • Session Authentication Tokens: Cryptographically signed JSON Web Tokens (JWT) generated upon successful authentication and stored locally in private device application storage (`SharedPreferences`).
  • Timestamps: Exact timestamps of visitor request creation, approval responses, gate entry, and gate exit.
  • Device Push Notification Token: An alphanumeric Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) device registration token generated by Google Play Services to route push alerts to your specific device.
No Advertising or Tracking SDKs: We do NOT embed third-party advertising SDKs, behavioral analytics trackers, or profiling algorithms in either mobile application.

5. How We Use Information

We use the data collected strictly for functional, security, and administrative purposes in connection with the operation of your residential society:

  • Authenticating Authorized Users: Verifying resident mobile numbers via OTP and verifying guard logins via Guard ID and hashed PIN.
  • Gate Access Control: Notifying residents when an unexpected or expected visitor arrives at the perimeter gate, showing the visitor's name, purpose, and gate photo for verification.
  • Security Audit Trails: Providing society administrators and residents with a reliable, timestamped history of gate entries, exits, deliveries, and vehicle movements for community safety.
  • Household Records Management: Enabling residents to maintain their own list of family members and authorized parking vehicles.
  • System Communications: Sending essential push notifications regarding visitor requests awaiting approval and critical society administrative announcements.

6. Account and Authentication Information

Our applications use role-based authentication mechanisms tailored to each user category:

Resident Authentication (NexGate Resident)

  • Residents register their mobile phone number with the society administration or submit an in-app registration request subject to administrator verification.
  • Authentication is passwordless: residents log in by receiving and verifying a secure One-Time Password (OTP) sent to their registered mobile phone number.
  • Upon successful OTP verification, a secure session token (JWT) is issued and stored in private device storage.

Guard Authentication (GreenGate Guard)

  • Security guards are provisioned directly by society management or security agencies with a unique Guard ID or registered phone number.
  • Guards authenticate using a 4-digit security PIN. PINs are salted and hashed using industry-standard bcrypt algorithms on the backend. PINs are never stored in plaintext.
  • Guards can change their 4-digit PIN directly within the Guard App after verifying their existing PIN.

7. Photos, Camera, and Media Usage

We treat camera access and photographic data with the highest level of care. Our two applications have strictly defined camera interactions:

GreenGate Guard App (Camera Access Disclosed)

  • Permission Requested: android.permission.CAMERA
  • Why the Camera is Used: The camera is used exclusively to take a front-facing verification photo of a visitor arriving at the society gate during the check-in workflow.
  • When the Camera is Accessed: The camera is activated only when the on-duty guard initiates a new visitor entry and navigates to the capture screen. The camera is never accessed in the background, when the screen is off, or outside the visitor entry workflow.
  • No Audio Recording: Audio recording is explicitly disabled (enableAudio: false). The app does not record or transmit sound.
  • Photo Transmission: The captured image is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS/TLS directly to our backend service and stored in our secure visitor photo repository (Cloudinary).
  • Intended Recipient: The visitor photo is displayed exclusively to the resident of the destination flat so they can visually confirm the visitor's identity before granting or denying gate access.

NexGate Resident App (Zero Camera Access)

  • The NexGate Resident App does NOT request or require android.permission.CAMERA.
  • Residents cannot take photos through the resident app. Residents only receive and view the visitor security photograph captured by the guard at the entrance gate.
  • The resident app caches visitor photos temporarily in sandboxed app memory using cached_network_image for fast rendering, which is purged in accordance with standard operating system cache management.

8. Location Information

No Location Collection: Neither the NexGate Resident App nor the GreenGate Guard App requests, collects, accesses, monitors, or transmits your device's geographical location (neither GPS, precise location, coarse network location, nor background location).

Neither android.permission.ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION nor android.permission.ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION is declared in the Android application manifests of either app. Society gate locations and apartment units are established administratively within the database, not via device GPS sensors.

9. Push Notifications

The NexGate Resident App requests the android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS permission (on Android 13 and above) to deliver timely gate alerts:

  • Incoming Visitor Approvals: Real-time alerts when a guest, delivery partner, or cab arrives at the security gate, allowing the resident to approve or deny entry immediately.
  • Visitor Decision Confirmations: Confirmation alerts regarding visitor entry or exit timestamps.
  • Society Notices: Important administrative broadcasts or emergency announcements issued by the resident's housing society committee.

Push Token Handling: To deliver these notifications, the resident's device obtains an anonymized Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) registration token from Google Play Services. This token is transmitted over HTTPS to our backend and stored in our secure database linked to the resident's user account. It is used solely to route incoming gate alerts to the resident's physical device. You may disable notifications at any time via Android System Settings.

10. How We Share Information

We do NOT sell, rent, monetize, or trade personal data to third parties, data brokers, or advertisers under any circumstances.

Information is disclosed only in the following strictly limited scenarios:

  • Within Your Residential Community: When a visitor arrives at the gate, their name, purpose, vehicle number, and photograph are displayed to the resident of the destination flat. Similarly, flat numbers and resident names are visible to authorized on-duty security guards to confirm the correct flat is contacted.
  • Society Administration: Authorized society administrators have access to society-level visitor entry logs, registered flat directories, and parking records for safety, audit, and community management purposes.
  • Infrastructure Service Providers: We share data with trusted cloud infrastructure providers who assist in operating our services under strict confidentiality and security obligations (detailed in Section 11).
  • Legal Compliance: We may disclose information if required to do so by applicable law, court order, governmental inquiry, or to protect the vital security and physical safety of residents and premises.

12. Data Storage and Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational security measures designed to safeguard personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction:

  • Encryption in Transit: All data transmitted between our mobile applications, backend APIs, and cloud services is encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS).
  • Credential Protection: Guard PINs and administrative passwords are cryptographic hashes generated using salted bcrypt. Plaintext credentials are never saved in the database.
  • Session Security: API requests require cryptographically signed JSON Web Tokens (JWT) with defined expiration periods.
  • Role-Based Access Control: Access to backend endpoints is strictly partitioned by user role (RESIDENT, GUARD, ADMIN). Guards cannot access resident management features, and residents cannot alter gate logs.
  • Database Hardening: Relational database queries use parameterized SQL inputs to prevent SQL injection vulnerabilities.
Security Notice: While we employ industry-standard technical measures designed to protect your information, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is completely impenetrable. We encourage users to keep their devices secure and promptly notify us of any suspected unauthorized access.

13. Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, provide our services, and satisfy community security requirements:

  • Resident Profiles & Household Records: Maintained for the duration of the resident's active residency in the registered society. When a resident moves out or requests account deletion, their profile, family records, and vehicle tags are deleted or archived by the society administrator.
  • Gate Security Visitor Logs & Photos: Visitor records (names, timestamps, and check-in photos) are retained for a standard operational period (typically between 30 to 180 days, depending upon the housing society's security bylaws and local regulatory requirements), after which records and photos are systematically purged.
  • Security Audit Trails: Access logs and critical security event records are retained in accordance with applicable statutory and security requirements.

14. Account and Data Deletion

In compliance with Google Play Store User Data policies, we provide transparent mechanisms for users to request the deletion of their accounts and associated personal data.

In-App Data Management (NexGate Resident App)

Residents can directly delete specific personal records inside the NexGate Resident App without contacting support:

  • Delete Family Members: Navigate to Profile & Settings → Household Family Members and tap the trash/delete icon next to any registered family member to immediately remove their record from our database.
  • Delete Vehicles: Navigate to Profile & Settings → Registered Vehicles and tap the delete icon next to any vehicle to immediately unregister that vehicle.

Request Full Account & Data Deletion

Because our platform is tied to physical gated apartment units and community safety records, complete resident account deletion requires verification to maintain accurate building tenancy lists.

To request the permanent deletion of your resident account, profile, household records, and associated data, you may submit a request using either of the following methods:

  1. Email Deletion Request: Send an email from your registered email address or mentioning your registered mobile number to our Privacy Team at:
    helpdesk@nex-build.com
    Please include your Full Name, Registered Mobile Number, Society Name, and Flat Number with the subject line "Account Deletion Request".
  2. Society Administrator Request: Contact your residential society management committee or estate administrator directly. Administrators possess direct administrative controls to delete resident profiles, flat linkages, and household accounts from the platform.

What Happens Upon Deletion:

  • Your resident user account, authentication records, and device push tokens are permanently removed from the active database.
  • Your associated family member records and vehicle registrations are deleted.
  • Past gate visitor logs previously approved or rejected by you are anonymized or retained only as non-attributable operational security records if required by local housing society regulations.
  • Deletion requests submitted via email are verified and processed within 30 calendar days.

Guard Accounts (GreenGate Guard App)

Guard accounts are institutional credentials created, assigned, and managed by society management or authorized security agencies. When a security guard concludes their assignment, the society administrator deactivates or permanently deletes their account through the administrative management dashboard.

15. Children's Privacy

Our applications are general-purpose community management tools designed for adult apartment residents, property owners, and adult security personnel. They are not directed to children under the age of 13 (or under 16/18 where applicable by local law).

We do not knowingly collect personal data directly from children under 13. While adult residents may choose to list family members (such as dependents) in their household section for apartment access purposes, such data is provided solely by the adult resident and is not used for profiling or marketing.

If you believe that a child under 13 has provided personal data directly to us without parental consent, please contact us at
helpdesk@nex-build.com, and we will take immediate steps to remove such information from our records.

16. Your Rights and Choices

Depending upon your jurisdiction and local privacy regulations, you may hold specific rights concerning your personal data:

  • Right to Access: You can review your profile details, registered flat information, family members, vehicles, and visitor history directly within the mobile application.
  • Right to Rectification: You can update or correct erroneous profile or household details directly within the app or by requesting your society administrator to update your flat assignment.
  • Right to Erasure: You have the right to request deletion of your account and personal data as described in Section 14.
  • Right to Restrict Notifications: You can manage or disable push notification permissions at any time through your Android device settings.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Where data processing is based on consent, you may withdraw your consent at any time by ceasing use of the application and requesting account deletion.
